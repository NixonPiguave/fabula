FÁBULA — PRIVACY
Notice version: 0.6.0 · 17 October 2026

WHAT STAYS ON YOUR COMPUTER
Fábula stores drafts, narration text, generated audio, projects, preferences,
voice profiles, pronunciation dictionary, transcriptions, playback positions
and downloaded models in %APPDATA%/Fábula. Exports go to your chosen location.
The portable application uses that same data folder. There is no Fábula account,
advertising or first-party telemetry implemented in this version.
Your interface language is stored locally in your preferences. On a new
installation it follows the installer choice, or a supported system language.

WHEN INFORMATION LEAVES YOUR COMPUTER
Voices marked as online send narration text, including samples, and synthesis
parameters to Microsoft. The provider also receives normal network connection
data such as your IP address. This is an independent integration with Edge's
reading service, not a service operated by Fábula. Microsoft's documentation
governs its data handling and retention:
https://privacy.microsoft.com/en-us/privacystatement
This notice does not claim Microsoft immediately deletes those texts.
Profile previews use an example phrase. Studio previews may use the beginning
of your editor text. Check the voice before synthesizing confidential content.
Windows and Piper voices process text locally without sending it to Microsoft.

EXTRACTION, RECOGNITION AND DOWNLOADS
Document extraction, OCR and Whisper transcription run locally. Dictation asks
for microphone permission and processes the recording on this computer. OCR,
Whisper and Piper modules are downloaded only when requested. These downloads
contact GitHub and Hugging Face servers, which receive network connection data.
Documents, text and recordings are not attached to module download requests.

DELETION AND RETENTION
Narrations remain until you delete them. Deleting from the library moves their
files to the Windows Recycle Bin; this is not secure deletion. Exported MP3 files
remain in their chosen locations. Deleting a project keeps its narrations.
Removing a voice model keeps audio already generated. Uninstalling preserves
the data folder to protect your library. To remove all local data, close Fábula
and delete %APPDATA%/Fábula, exports and, where applicable, the Recycle Bin.
Do not upload this data folder to a public repository or attach it to issues.

SCOPE
This notice describes this local version. Distributors adding servers,
analytics or synchronization must disclose those changes and meet applicable
requirements. Online provider terms are separate from Fábula's code license.
See also USAGE_NOTICES.txt. Original third-party licenses remain in their
original language; translated explanations do not replace those texts.
