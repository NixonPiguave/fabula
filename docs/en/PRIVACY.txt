FÁBULA — PRIVACY
19 October 2026

LOCAL DATA
Fábula stores manuscripts, drafts, audio, projects, voice profiles, preferences,
transcripts, bookmarks and downloaded models in %APPDATA%/Fábula. The portable
uses the same folder. Exports are saved to your chosen location. The installer
sets the initial interface language only for new profiles. Fábula has no user
accounts, advertising or implemented first-party telemetry.

PROCESSING AND CONNECTIONS
Windows SAPI and Piper narration, document extraction, OCR and Whisper speech
recognition run on your computer. Fábula does not send manuscripts or recordings
to a synthesis or recognition service. Studio previews can use the beginning
of your draft; profile previews use a sample sentence. Both run locally.
Dictation requests microphone permission and processes the recording locally.
Optional OCR, Whisper and Piper components are downloaded when requested.
Downloads contact GitHub and Hugging Face servers, which receive ordinary
connection information such as your IP address. Manuscripts and recordings
are not attached to these download requests. External links open in your
browser, subject to its policies.

RETENTION AND DELETION
Narrations remain until you delete them. Library deletion moves files to the
Windows Recycle Bin; this is not secure erasure. Exported MP3 files remain in
their chosen location. Removing a project preserves its narrations. Removing
a voice preserves generated audio. Uninstalling preserves your library. To
remove all local data, close Fábula and delete %APPDATA%/Fábula, your exports
and, where applicable, the Recycle Bin contents. Do not publish your data
folder or attach it to public issues.

SCOPE
This notice describes the official distribution. Distributors adding servers,
analytics or synchronization must disclose those changes and meet applicable
obligations. See USAGE_NOTICES.txt.
